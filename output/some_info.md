Some information about [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipium](Municipium). Themuneraand the citizenship and its rights and protections were specific to the community. No matter where a person lived, at home or abroad, or what his status or class, he was a citizen of the locality in which he was born. The distinguishingcharacteristicof themunicipiumwasself-governance. Like any ancient city-state, themunicipiumwas created by an official act ofsynoecism, or founding. This act removed the sovereignty and independence from the signatory local communities, replacing them with the jurisdiction of a common government. This government was then called theres publica('public affair'), or in the Greek world thekoinon('common affair').
